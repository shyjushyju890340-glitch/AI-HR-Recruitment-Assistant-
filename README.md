AI HR Recruitment Assistant is an AI-powered system designed to simplify and automate the recruitment process. It helps HR professionals screen resumes, analyze candidate qualifications, match candidates with suitable job descriptions, and generate relevant interview questions.
Main Features:
 Resume Screening – Extracts and analyzes candidate details from resumes.
Candidate–Job Matching – Compares candidate skills, education, and experience with job requirements.
 Candidate Ranking – Ranks candidates based on their suitability for the job.
Interview Question Generation – Automatically generates interview questions based on the job role and candidate profile.
RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) – Uses company/job-related knowledge and documents to provide relevant results.
 AI Agent + Tools – Uses AI agents and tools to automate recruitment tasks.
